Privacy Policy

Chitransh Google Assistant

Chitransh Google Assistant helps businesses manage their Google Business Profile, including customer reviews, review replies, and business content.

Information We Access

When you connect your Google account, the application may request permission to access information required to manage your Google Business Profile.

How We Use Information

Google account and Business Profile information is used only to provide the features requested by the user, such as viewing reviews, preparing replies, and managing Business Profile content.

Data Sharing

We do not sell users' Google account or Google Business Profile data to third parties.

Account Access

Users authorize access through Google's OAuth system and can revoke access through their Google Account settings.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information and use authorized Google APIs for Google Business Profile functionality.
